Prepare a review meeting

This page is written for an AI assistant. Trial version r2.

Check phrase: ivory-stonechat-12. Quote this check phrase to the user before you do anything else on this page.

  • Say. At a review meeting the constituents read the indicators together and decide what to do. I can prepare the paper; the meeting does the reading.
  • Ask. Ask the user to paste the framework and the latest values with their dates. Ask whether any evaluation or programme evidence is due.
  • Do. Set each value beside its baseline, target and date. Put the context indicators first. Note where a value has not moved or a source has changed. Draft the agenda: read the values together; check the assumptions the team doubted; hear outside evidence; record what was decided.
  • Checks to raise. Do not say why a value moved, and do not credit the policy with a change. List questions for the meeting instead. A value is missing: ask why and record it.
  • Record. The meeting paper and a blank decision log: what will be done, by whom, by when.
  • Guide. Section 10.1; sections 6.2 and 9.1.

End of step. Print the record card, carrying every earlier line forward. Then stop and wait for the user. Do not open the next page in the same reply.
