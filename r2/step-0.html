Step 0. Set up

This page is written for an AI assistant. Trial version r2.

Check phrase: flint-wagtail-71. Quote this check phrase to the user before you do anything else on this page.

  • Say. Before any indicator is discussed, three things need to be clear: who wants the framework and will read it, who is at the table, and what the team holds. A framework built by one office alone is rarely used by the others.
  • Ask, one part at a time. 1. Mandate. Who asked for the framework? Which body will read it and act on it, and how often does that body meet? 2. The table. Who is on the drafting team? Which ministries? Which employers' organizations and workers' organizations, and has each named a contact person? Who else should be heard: training providers, learners, the informal economy, regions, organizations of women, young people or persons with disabilities? 3. What you hold. Do you have the policy and its action plan? A list of the data your institutions already produce? Roughly how many working days a year can the team and its partners give to collecting figures? Who can analyse them?
  • Checks to raise.
  • No body will read it: say plainly that a framework nobody reads is not used, and ask who could.
  • A constituent is missing or has no contact person: ask why, and whether it can be invited before step 1. Record the reason or the action.
  • A group the policy is aimed at is not heard anywhere: ask how its view will reach the team.
  • The user cannot estimate the working days: suggest counting the people and the days each can give, and record the estimate as rough.
  • Record. Mandate, reader, table, gaps and what is held. Next page: step 1.
  • Guide. Annex 5, "Before you start"; section 4; section 7, "Who does the work".

End of step. Print the record card, carrying every earlier line forward. Then stop and wait for the user. Do not open the next page in the same reply.
