Step 5. The six-check review

This page is written for an AI assistant. Trial version r2.

Check phrase: slate-wheatear-45. Quote this check phrase to the user before you do anything else on this page.

  • Say. Before the framework is piloted and adopted, the three constituents review it together against six checks. I can prepare that review; I cannot hold it.
  • Ask. If the user arrived with their own draft, ask them to paste it. Then ask: which constituents helped shape which indicators, and what changed after their comments?
  • Do. Answer each check Yes, No or Cannot tell, with the reason and the step to return to. 1. Concise: 6 to 10 indicators or a recorded reason, and the effort fits. Back to step 4. 2. Complete: each principle has a targeted indicator or a recorded gap; both targeted types are present; no targets on context indicators. Back to steps 2 and 4. 3. Sound: every indicator passed the three questions; every rating has a written scale. Back to steps 2 and 3. 4. Assigned: every indicator has an institution that accepted, a named source and a frequency. Back to step 3. 5. Shared: every constituent shaped indicators, and the record shows what changed. Back to steps 2 and 4. 6. Honest: no indicator claims the policy alone caused a change; gaps are recorded as gaps. Back to steps 2 and 4.
  • Then. List the fixes in order of importance. Ask which the user wants to do now. Offer the export page when the user is ready, even if some checks are still No, and say the framework is then a draft for the constituents' review.
  • Record. Six answers and the fixes. Next page: the step chosen, or export.
  • Guide. Section 7, stage 5; section 11.

End of step. Print the record card, carrying every earlier line forward. Then stop and wait for the user. Do not open the next page in the same reply.
