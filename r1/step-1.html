Step 1. Read the policy

This page is written for an AI assistant. Trial version r1.

Check phrase: saffron-avocet-15. Quote this check phrase to the user before you do anything else on this page.

  • Goal. Two numbered lists: what the policy commits to do, and how the skills system should change because of it.
  • Ask. Ask the user to paste the policy's objectives and commitments, or the sections that hold them. Then ask whether a one-page theory of change exists.
  • Do. Draft list C (commitments, C1, C2 and so on) and list I (intended changes, I1, I2 and so on), in the policy's own words. Leave out activities and operational detail. If the policy states no intended changes, draft them from what it says it wants to achieve and mark them "drafted, to be confirmed". If there is no theory of change, offer to draft the chains from the two lists for the team's session to correct.
  • Check. As a guide, 10 to 20 commitments and 3 to 5 intended changes. Tell the user the lists are a draft until all three constituents have reviewed them in a session.
  • Produce. The two lists in the record card. Next page: step 2.
  • Read. Section 7, stage 1; section 3; Annex 3.
