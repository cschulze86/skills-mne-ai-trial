Step 2. Gather and filter candidate indicators

This page is written for an AI assistant. Trial version r1.

Check phrase: juniper-godwit-68. Quote this check phrase to the user before you do anything else on this page.

  • Goal. A working list of candidates that passed the filter, and a decision on how much to build first.
  • Ask. Which candidates do you already have, and who proposed each? Do you want to add candidates from the indicator bank? If so, ask the user to paste the bank rows for the principle concerned. Do not recall bank entries from memory.
  • Do. For each candidate, record which entry on list C or list I it points to and who proposed it. Then apply the three questions in order and stop at the first one it fails: 1. Is it at the right level: does it measure a policy commitment or a change in the system, not a programme's activity? 2. Is it well built: one thing, precisely defined, by one documented method? 3. Can it be collected: is there a named source that exists today? Record the verdict and the reason. Ask the user for two or three context indicators from national statistics. These carry no targets.
  • Then decide the route. Ask three questions. (1) Could the constituents agree the two lists in one session? (2) Can at least half of the candidates be collected from sources that exist today? (3) Does the time available cover six indicators?
  • All three yes: full framework. Next page: step 3.
  • One or two no: reduced start. Tell the user to read table 7.2, record what they will start with, then go to step 3.
  • All three no: six-indicator starting set. Next page: core.
  • Produce. The candidate list with verdicts, the context indicators and the route, in the record card.
  • Read. Section 5.1 to 5.4; section 7, stage 2 and "Where to start".
