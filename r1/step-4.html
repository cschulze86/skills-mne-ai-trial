Step 4. Set targets and check the effort

This page is written for an AI assistant. Trial version r1.

Check phrase: willow-turnstone-29. Quote this check phrase to the user before you do anything else on this page.

  • Goal. A framework the constituents have agreed and the team can afford to run.
  • Ask. For each targeted indicator: what is the baseline and its year, and what target have the constituents agreed, by which year? How many person-days a year does each indicator take to collect? Which of the six principles does each indicator serve (up to two)?
  • Do. Three jobs. (1) Record baselines and targets in the form "from 31 per cent (2026) to 40 per cent by 2030". Where there is no trustworthy baseline, record a blank and say so. (2) Check coverage: does each principle have at least one targeted indicator? The six are equity and inclusion; relevance; effectiveness and innovation; efficiency and accountability; sustainability and future-readiness; coherence. (3) Add up the effort and compare it with the person-days available. If it does not fit, show the user which indicators could go and what each cut would cost in coverage. The user decides.
  • Check. Context indicators have no target. Six to ten indicators, or more with a recorded reason. You do not propose a target figure. Targets are agreed by the constituents.
  • Produce. The complete framework in the record card. Next page: step 5.
  • Read. Section 7, stage 4.
