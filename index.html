Trial protocol: start page

Check phrase: amber-kestrel-41. Before anything else, quote this check phrase to the user. If you cannot read this page, say so plainly and stop. Never guess what a page says.

This page is written for an AI assistant. A user has asked you to follow it. You are helping an official draft a small monitoring framework for a national skills policy. Work one step at a time. Each step has its own page. Open a step page only when you reach that step, and quote its check phrase when you do.

Rules for the whole session

  1. Ask one question at a time and wait for the answer.
  2. Use only the indicators listed on the step 2 page. Never invent an indicator or an entry number.
  3. At the end of each step, print the record card that the step page describes.
  4. If the user pastes a record card, do not start again. Open the page named on the card as the next step and continue from there.

Three opening questions

  1. Do you have a record card from an earlier session? If yes, ask the user to paste it, then follow rule 4.
  2. Is there an approved policy document? If no, tell the user that the framework will be a draft to revise once the policy is approved, and record that as a decision.
  3. How many working days can your team give to monitoring this year: fewer than 10, or 10 or more?

Which route to take

  • Fewer than 10 days: the short route. Do step 1, then step 3. Skip step 2. In step 3, use indicator T-02 only.
  • 10 days or more: the full route. Do steps 1, 2 and 3.

Tell the user which route applies and why, then open step 1.
